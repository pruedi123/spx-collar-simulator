What the model is showing

The app buys a one-year SPX put and sells a one-year SPX call at the selected strikes. Contract sizing uses the entered SPX level, a $100 multiplier, beta-adjusted target coverage, and whole-contract rounding.

Model premiums use Black-Scholes with separate put and call volatility assumptions. Entered market quotes override the initial premiums and imply separate starting volatilities for interim illustration. A net debit is financed and a net credit is invested at the entered interest rate. Dividends accumulate as cash.

Historical resampling uses contiguous three-month blocks of Shiller monthly S&P Composite price returns. Ordinary returns are converted to log returns before recentering and compounding.

Historical replay reproduces the index path, not historical option prices. Interim option values are hypothetical marks. Expiration intrinsic value is the more reliable educational result.

The strong-market panel compares the same portfolio with and without the collar. Upside given up equals the unhedged ending value minus the collared ending value, after dividends and premium financing. It is not simply the call's intrinsic value.

Data sources, contract mechanics, and alternatives

Cboe SPX specifications describe the $100 multiplier, European exercise, cash settlement, and expiration conventions. Traditional AM-settled SPX and PM-settled SPXW can use different official settlement references.

OCC's options disclosure explains index-option risks, including the risk that a securities portfolio will not move exactly with the index used for the hedge.

Long-run monthly S&P Composite price levels are derived from Robert Shiller's public market dataset. Recent replay observations are sampled from the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis FRED S&P 500 series. Embedded data are static through 2025.

This app models one static one-year collar. Cboe collar benchmarks also use rolling structures, including quarterly puts with monthly calls and three-month puts with three-month calls. Those alternatives can have materially different cost, liquidity, and reset behavior.

Educational illustration only. This is a hypothetical static one-year SPX collar, not an executable quote, forecast, recommendation, or assurance of a portfolio floor. Entered quotes are not validated against an exchange. The model excludes commissions, exchange fees, market impact, liquidity constraints, counterparty and clearing risk, taxes, accounting treatment, collateral and margin requirements, and management decisions before expiration. SPX options are cash settled, so settlement timing and the official settlement value can differ from the portfolio's value. Historical observations and simulated scenario frequencies do not predict future results.
